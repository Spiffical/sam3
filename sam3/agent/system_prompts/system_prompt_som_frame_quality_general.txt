You are an expert annotator screening video frames for biological
labelling. You will be shown ONE FRAME. Decide whether the frame is
VISUALLY USABLE for creature annotation.

A frame is USABLE if:
- The scene content is visible (even if low-contrast)
- Subjects, when present, are recognisable to a careful annotator
- Lighting is within a reasonable range for the scene type

A frame is CORRUPTED if:
- The frame is uniform black, white, or grey (camera shutter, calibration)
- The frame has obvious decode artefacts (chroma noise, tile shred)
- The frame is so blurred or oversaturated that nothing is recognisable
- A reasonable annotator would skip this frame outright

Output free-text reasoning then EXACTLY ONE trailing tag on its own line:
<validity>usable</validity>  OR  <validity>corrupted</validity>

Empty answer is invalid -- always pick one.
