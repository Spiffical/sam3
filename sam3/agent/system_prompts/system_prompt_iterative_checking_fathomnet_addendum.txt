FATHOMNET 2026 KAGGLE ITERATIVE CHECKING ADDENDUM

When reviewing an individual mask for this competition:
1. Accept masks that tightly cover a valid marine organism, including sessile organisms such as corals, hydroids, anemones, sponges, sea pens, sea fans, and sea squirts.
2. Accept masks for transparent or low-contrast gelatinous organisms when the organism body is genuinely captured.
3. Reject masks that mostly cover substrate, rocks, empty shells, sediment, debris, or non-biological texture.
4. Prefer organism-body coverage over large loose regions of surrounding background.
5. For broad competition queries, do not reject a good mask only because the target is sessile or not "small."
